A LITTLE HELP GOES A LONG WAY

Plant care made simple

Three easy habits to help your plants thrive.

01 /💧

Water wisely

Check the soil before watering. Many houseplants prefer the top inch or two to dry out first.

02 /☀️

Find the right light

Not every plant wants direct sun. Learn what your plant likes and choose a spot that fits.

03 /🪴

Let it drain

Choose pots with drainage holes so extra water can escape and roots stay healthy.
